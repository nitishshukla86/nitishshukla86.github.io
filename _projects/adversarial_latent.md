## Research Goal
Most adversarial examples add imperceptible pixel-level noise. This work asks whether semantically
meaningful adversarial examples can be produced by perturbing a generative model's latent space instead.

## How it works
- **Latent perturbations.** Adversarial perturbations are injected into the latent space of a generative
  model, so the resulting changes are structured rather than pixel noise.
- **Probing decision boundaries.** The approach shows how a network's decision boundaries can be probed
  through structured manipulations of latent representations.
